# Guidelines for single cell RNA sequencing analysis of Eosinophils 
### This is a resource for our analysis of Guidelines for scRNAseq analysis of eosinophils from the Arnold lab.

#### All data used are accessible under the specifiec accessory number on GEO or ArrayExpress. Additionally, datafiels generated for this study can be found on zenodo.org 

### Code description: 
**1. Packages and functions:**
- 1.1.Packages.R: All packages that need to be loaded
- 1.2.Functions_Seurat_integration.R: Functions to generate Seurat objects from cell/count matrices generated by different methods
- 1.3.Functions_gene_expression.R: Functions for DEG analysis and plotting of genes of interest in a heatmap
- 1.4.Functions_MALAT1_threshold_empty_droplets_Clarke_Bader_etal.R: Function to calculate the MALAT1/Malat1 threshold to distinguish real cells from empty droplets, function from Clarke and Bader et al 2014;
- 1.5.Functions_ambient_RNA_plotting.R: Function to plot the percentage of cells within a certain cell type clusters that expresses a certain set of marker genes; adapted from the decontX vignette
- 1.6.Functions_Doublet_detection.R: Function to apply upper gene cutoff as the 99th percentile of the number of genes
- 1.7.Functions_cell_type_prop.R: Functions to plot cell type proportions between conditions
  
**2. Data annotation:**
  - 2.1.Annotation_Hs_tumor_NAT_GSE282765.R: Integration and annotation of NAT and tumor from Hs CRC patients; data from GSE282765, 7 samples each
  - 2.2.Annotation_Hs_PB_GSE282765.R: Integration and annotation of PB from healthy individuals and CRC patients; data from GSE282765, 9 healthy, 7 CRC;
  - 2.3.Annotation_Mm_tumor_colon_GSE282765.R: Integration and annotation of tumor, NAT, disseminated from AKPS CRC Mm, and healthy Mm colon; data from GSE282765, 1 sample each;
  - 2.4.Annotation_Mm_blood_bm_GSE282765.R: Integration and annotation of blood and BM of AKPS CRC mice and healthy mice; data from GSE282765, 1 sample each;
  - 2.5.Annotation_Mm_il5tg_GSE182001.R: Integration and annotation of BM, blood, spleen, colon, SI and stomach from il5-tg mice at steady state; data from GSE182001, 1 sample each;
  - 2.6.Annotation_Mm_eos_subtypes_il5tg_GSE182001.R: Integration and annotation of eosinophil subtypes from il5-tg mice at steady state; data from GSE182001, 1 sample each;
  - 2.7.Annotation_Hs_PB_EoE_healthy_GSE256088.R: Integration and annotation of Hs PB healthy individuals and EoE patients; data from GSE256088, 3 samples each;
  - 2.8.Annotation_Hs_PB_asthma_healthy_E-MTAB-14010.R: Integration and annotation of Hs PB from healthy individuals and Asthma patients; data from E-MTAB-14010, 3 samples each;
  - 2.9.Annotation_Mm_BM_GSM7919060.R: Annotation of Mm healthy BM; data from GSM7919060, 1 sample;
  - 2.10.Annotation_Hs_PB_asthma_healthy_GSE276583.R: Integration and annotation of Hs PB from healthy individuals and Asthma severe adn mild patients; data from GSE276583, 4 healthy eos, 3 healthy MNCs, 4 severe, 3 mild; 
  - 2.11.Annotation_Mm_liver_GSE216189.R: Integration and annotation of Mm liver from healthy mice and mice with metastasis in the liver through AKPS intrasplenic injection; data from GSE216189, 1 healthy, 11 metastatic; 
  - 2.12.Annotation_Hs_Eso_Duod_EoE_GSE175930.R: Integration and annotation of Hs esophagus and duodenum from EoE patients with active disease and in remission; data from GSE175930, 4 remission, 5 active; 

**3. Gene counts per cell type**

  - 4.3.1.Empty_doplets_by_BD_pipeline_Mm.R: Annotation of cells from BD automatic pipeline based on BD forced annotation, data from GSE282765; Mm BM and blood from healthy and CRC, 1 each; 
  - 4.3.2.Empty_droplets_by_EmptyDrops_Mm.R: Running of EmptyDrops algorithm and annotation of resulting cells based on BD forced annotation, data from GSE282765; Mm BM and blood from healthy and CRC, 1 each; 
  - 4.3.3.Empty_droplets_by_Malat1_threshold_Mm.R: Running of MALAT1 threshold algorithm and annotation of resulting cells based on BD forced annotation, data from GSE282765; Mm BM and blood from healthy and CRC, 1 each;
  - 4.3.4.Empty_doplets_n_cells_across_approaches_Mm.R: Assessment of the quality of additional eosinophils from BD forced, EmtpyDrops and MALAT1 threshold; data from GSE282765; Mm BM and blood from healthy and CRC, 1 each;

**6. Ambient RNA detection and decontamination**

  - 6.2.2.Ambient_RNA_DecontX_quant_decont_Mm.R: Quantification of ambient RNA and decontamination of count matrix using the DecontX workflow; data from GSE282765; Mm colon healthy, tumor, NAT and dissemianted, 1 each;

  - 6.2.4.Ambient_RNA_decont_cell_type_marker_expr_comp_Mm.R: Comparison of cell type marker detection between decontamination tools, data from GSE282765; Mm colon healthy, tumor, NAT and dissemianted, 1 each;

  - 6.3.2.Ambient_RNA_DecontX_quant_Hs.R: Quantification of ambient RNA using the DecontX workflow; data from GSE282765; Hs NAT and tumor, 7 each; 
  - 6.3.3.Ambient_RNA_SoupX__quant_Hs.R: Quantification of ambient RNA using the SoupX workflow; data from GSE282765; Hs NAT and tumor, 7 each; 
  - 6.4.1.Ambient_RNA_quantification_plotting_Hs_Mm.R: Plotting of quantified contamination summarising all three tools and all datasets, data from GSE282765; Hs NAT and tumor, 7 each; Mm colon healthy, tumor, NAT and dissemianted, 1 each;
